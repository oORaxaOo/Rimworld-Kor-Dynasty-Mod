# KOR: DYNASTIE — balance doctrine

The values below are balancing principles, not claims about final numeric stats.

## Force tiers

| Tier | Intended frequency | Strength | Counterplay |
|---|---|---|---|
| Line troops | Common | Reliable training and serviceable gear | Ordinary weapons, cover, numbers and terrain |
| Specialists | Occasional | Role-specific utility | Exploit gaps in their specialization |
| Kor elite | Rare | Better equipment and skills | Attrition, focus fire, flanking, suppression/mental pressure where applicable, and supply costs |
| Strategic support | Event-gated | Major tactical effect | Telegraphing, cooldowns, limited availability and meaningful consequences |

## Fairness requirements

- No universal immunity to damage types.
- No arbitrary perfect accuracy or unlimited ammunition.
- Equipment value must be reflected in trade value, crafting costs or encounter points.
- Elite pawns remain vulnerable to injuries, bleeding, exhaustion and environmental hazards.
- Threats should scale through encounter composition and scarcity, not only raw stat inflation.
- The player must have understandable warning and meaningful counterplay before major escalations.

## Test scenarios

1. Equal-point Kor line squad versus a comparable vanilla outlander squad.
2. Small elite detachment versus a larger, lower-tech force.
3. Kor troops defending a settlement against a raid.
4. Kor equipment against common weapons at short, medium and long range.
5. Injury, death, retreat and post-combat recovery.
6. Repeated raids over a long save to detect runaway wealth/threat escalation.

Record pawn count, gear value, injuries, deaths, battle duration and log errors. Tune after real gameplay tests; do not use static XML checks as a substitute for combat balance.
